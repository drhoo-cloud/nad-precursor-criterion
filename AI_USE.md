# Artificial intelligence use

Supporting record for the AI declaration in the article.

**Tool.** Claude (Anthropic).

**What is the authors' own.** The equations, the parameters, the code and the interpretation are the authors' own. Every numerical output was verified by the authors against `verify_values.py` in this archive.

## Where the model assisted

- Laying out Figures 3 and 4 as two panels each, and aligning the shading of the elasticity panel with that of Figure 3.
- Computing the entries of the measured-elasticity column of the output-ratio table (`data/table_S7_output_ratio.csv`).
- Drafting the figure legends.

## Where the model was used to check

The model was used to check every numerical claim against the primary sources. That audit produced three corrections:

1. The ceramide concentration of §5.3 was corrected from micromolar to millimolar.
2. The direction of the fluorescence-lifetime change in §6.4 was reversed.
3. The tissue nicotinamide measurements now cited for M₀ were located.

**Each correction was checked against the source by the authors before it was made.**

The same audit reconciled the citation numbering of the deposited record with the article, and added the provenance notes to the enzyme-conditions table (Table B.1).

## Restructuring for this journal

The article was reorganised from an earlier version for submission to *Biochemical Pharmacology*, whose policy limits supplementary data to video and audio. The model assisted in that restructuring: moving the derivation, the enzyme-conditions table, the sensitivity sweep and the branch-assignment table into in-article Appendices A to C, relocating the remaining supporting material into this archive, and renumbering the references accordingly. The authors reviewed the result in full.

## Scope of this declaration

No generative image model was used. The graphical abstract was built from geometric primitives, typeset text and a curve computed from the constants of Table 2; no third-party image was used. No new experimental data are reported in the article, and the model was not used to generate, impute or alter any data value.
